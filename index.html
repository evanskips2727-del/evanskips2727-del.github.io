Machine learning · NexAfrica internship

Customer Churn Prediction Pipeline

End-to-end pipeline on 7,043 telecom accounts to find who is about to leave and why, built so retention teams can intervene early.

  • Found that 0–5 month accounts, month-to-month contracts, electronic-check payers and fibre-optic customers carry the highest risk.
  • Engineered features (TenureGroup, TotalServices, CostPerMonthOfTenure), expanding to 35 model-ready dimensions.
  • Handled a 73.5% / 26.5% imbalance with SMOTE inside an imblearn pipeline to prevent leakage; tuned with 5-fold GridSearchCV for recall.
  • Compared Logistic Regression, Random Forest and XGBoost; best model reached 76.2% recall and 0.824 ROC-AUC.
